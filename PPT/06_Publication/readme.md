## What this paper establishes

This paper proves that Ternary Logic's Epistemic Hold and Provisional Permission Token architecture renders two of the three ASI non-cooperation threat classes architecturally unreachable:

- **Unauthorized action** - closed by PPT-gated execution and the formal invariant: if no valid PPT exists, no protected action proceeds
- **Unauthorized silence** - closed by DITL's NULL spacer, which forces the Epistemic Hold as a physically observable and non-executable state
- **Malicious compliance** - identified as the open frontier requiring semantic alignment research, with three concrete research directions proposed

The paper synthesizes findings from five independent research assessments spanning asynchronous circuit theory, formal systems theory, cryptographic verification, thermodynamic limits of computation, and distributed systems impossibility results. Their convergence on identical boundaries constitutes the strongest available evidence that those boundaries are real.

## Version 1.1 additions

Three extensions derived from post-draft review:

1. **Tiered Epistemic Hold architecture** for distributed multi-cluster deployment, addressing the FLP asynchrony boundary with localized watchdog shards and latency-tolerant reconciliation windows
2. **Active defense mechanisms** against temporal baseline corruption, including epistemic resets using historically snapshotted reference models and deliberate unannounced perturbation testing
3. **Thermodynamic profiling** as a candidate semantic circuit breaker, converting the thermodynamic asymmetry between degradation and detection into a measurable physical observable
